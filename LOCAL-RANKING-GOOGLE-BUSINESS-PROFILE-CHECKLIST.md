# Brodley Locksmiths — Google Business Profile Checklist

Use this after the Local Ranking & Crawl Quality Update is live.

## Main positioning
- Keep the primary category as **Locksmith**.
- Do not add a vehicle locksmith category, because Brodley Locksmiths does not offer vehicle locksmith work.
- Use the website URL: `https://www.brodley-locksmiths.com`.

## Services to prioritise in the profile
Add or review services around planned property locksmith work:

- Lock changes
- Lock upgrades
- Anti-snap / high-security cylinder upgrades
- Euro cylinder replacement
- uPVC door repairs
- Door alignment and adjustment
- Key safe fitting
- Security surveys
- Gate / side access security
- Landlord lock changes
- Small business lock changes

## Review request angle
When asking for reviews, never script fake wording. But it helps when genuine reviews naturally mention:

- the service carried out
- the town or area
- whether the job was tidy, helpful and professional
- planned work such as lock changes, uPVC repairs, key safes or security upgrades

Examples of useful genuine review context: “lock change in Clacton”, “uPVC door repair in Frinton”, “key safe fitted for my mum”, “security cylinder upgrade”, “landlord lock change”.
