# Brodley Locksmiths — Implementation Phases

1. **Technical/indexing (Weeks 1–2)** — audit indexing exports, redirects, sitemap, canonicals, security dependencies, API form delivery. Gate: no critical vulnerabilities known in production; key pages accessible; accurate indexing data recorded.
2. **Google Business Profile and citations (Weeks 2–3)** — real service categories, genuine reviews, photos, trading hours, listings alignment. Gate: profile is accurate and complete; repeatable review/photo workflow.
3. **Trust and conversion (Weeks 3–4)** — human About page, homepage clarity, reliable quote flow, priority mobile CTAs. Gate: real-world customer test passes on phone and desktop.
4. **Core service and local SEO authority (Weeks 5–7)** — differentiated guides for lock changes, uPVC repair, cylinders, key safes, real local examples without invented jobs. Gate: unique helpful content, retained canonical paths, comparison of service performance.
5. **AI and alternative search visibility (Weeks 7–8)** — crawl eligibility, structured data, Bing, Apple, honest answer-led content. Gate: no crawler blocks, consistent business data, webmaster properties verified.
6. **Business growth and measurement (Weeks 9–12)** — proof-led case studies, local partnerships, weekly revenue and enquiries scorecard, measure branded search demand. Gate: monthly comparison of leads, job bookings and revenue by source.

**Important:** Deployment scheduling should follow technical readiness and business data, not force an update each week.
